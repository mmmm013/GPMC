## Purpose

This draft converts the internal K-KUT package foundation into simple buyer-facing package cards and BOT intake prompts for k-kut.com.

This document does not replace GPMC doctrine.

GPMC remains the source authority.

k-kut.com receives simplified buyer-facing copy only.

## Storefront Language Rule

Buyer-facing copy must be:

- short
- clear
- emotionally useful
- easy to choose from
- not overloaded with internal doctrine
- not overpromising
- not confusing KKs, sKs, HUGs, and CIs

Internal terms such as Track, PIX, LT-PIX, lineage, registry, KKr, and CI should not appear in normal customer-facing package cards unless the buyer is in an admin, partner, or advanced review context.

## Core Storefront Introduction

K-KUTs are short, personal audio gifts made to carry real feeling.

Choose a moment, tell us who it is for, and we help shape the right K-KUT or HUG for the message.

Use K-KUT for birthdays, anniversaries, romance, apologies, encouragement, gratitude, and moments that deserve more than a text.
