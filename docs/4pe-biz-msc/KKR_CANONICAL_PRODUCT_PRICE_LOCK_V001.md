# KKr Canonical Product / Price Lock V001

**Status:** PERMANENT CANONICAL LAW  
**Owner and final authority:** GD / G Putnam Music, LLC  
**Currency:** USD  
**Source authority:** GPMC

## Canonical product and price law

| Product / structure | Price |
|---|---:|
| Vocal-note or typed-message add-on | $0.99 |
| mKUT | $1.99 |
| sBLK | $4.99 |
| BLK | $7.99 |
| Promotional HUG | $11.99 |
| Standard HUG | $14.99 |

## Structural law

An **sBLK** is one identified structural segment of a BLK, such as Verse 1a, Verse 1b, Verse 2a, or Verse 2b.

An **mKUT is not an sBLK**.

Storefront labels, campaign names, Stripe product names, screenshots, Word files, and historical Payment Links do not redefine this structure or these prices.

## Forbidden equivalences

- mKUT must never be classified as sBLK.
- mKUT must never be priced at $4.99.
- sBLK must never be priced at $1.99.
- BLK must never be priced at $4.99.
- Historical Stripe links are not pricing authority.
- Customer checkout is valid only when its visible product and amount match this law.

## Change control

Any amendment requires an explicit GD decision, a new numbered lock version, a GPMC source commit, a matching K-KUT machine lock, and a passing build audit. Silent edits are prohibited.
