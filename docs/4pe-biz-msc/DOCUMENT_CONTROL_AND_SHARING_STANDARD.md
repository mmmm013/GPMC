# Document Control and Sharing Standard

Status: Foundation Standard  
Owner: G Putnam Music, LLC  
Environment: 4PE-BIZ-MSC → KKr-BIZ-MSC → GPMx Operations

## Core Rule

GPMC is the source authority for company documents, doctrine, package specifications, audio lineage, operational standards, and review records.

Microsoft Word, OneDrive, SharePoint, Google Docs, email attachments, PDFs, and shared cloud links are not source authority.

They may be used only as editors, exports, review copies, or delivery copies.

## Ownership Rule

GD / Greg Putnam and G Putnam Music, LLC retain direct ownership, access, and control over company documents.

No external platform may become the authority for whether a document exists, who may edit it, who may receive it, or what version is official.

## Sharing Rule

Official documents live in GPMC first.

When sharing is needed:
1. Create or update the source document in GPMC.
2. Export a copy if needed.
3. Send or share the copy.
4. Preserve the GPMC source as authority.

## Microsoft / SharePoint Rule

Microsoft Word, OneDrive, and SharePoint are not trusted as operational authority.

Do not rely on Microsoft cloud sharing as the company document-control system.

If Microsoft blocks, redirects, requires upload, forces account storage, or interferes with access, stop and use GPMC source plus exported copy instead.

GD may share documents or exports with unpredictable contacts at any time.

The system must support direct export and direct sending without requiring a platform-specific sharing gate.

The official source remains GPMC.
