## CI Definition

CI = Cost Item.

A CI is not merely an asset type.

A CI is the transaction-state of an inventory item after purchase or selection causes it to become an item purchased.

Before purchase:
- inventory item
- selectable item
- offerable item
- package option

At purchase:
- the transaction converts the selected inventory item into a CI.

After purchase:
- the CI carries purchaser, price, delivery, rights/use case, source lineage, and fulfillment proof.

## Asset-to-CI Chain

Song  
→ Track  
→ PIX  
→ LT-PIX  
→ KK  
→ sK  
→ inventory item  
→ purchase / transaction  
→ CI

LT-PIXs, KKs, and sKs are CI-capable inventory items.

They become CIs only when the user transaction converts them into purchased Cost Items.

## Short-LT-PIX / MC Shorties Rule

Short songs use the same authority spine.

Short song  
→ Track  
→ PIX  
→ Short-LT-PIX  
→ KK  
→ sK  
→ inventory item  
→ purchased CI

MC Shorties are not exceptions. They are first-class Short-LT-PIX assets.

Short-LT-PIXs may use fewer BLKs:
- BLK1 only
- BLK1 + BLK2
- BLK1 + BLK2 + BLK3
- more only when the audio truly supports it

Most LT-PIXs may use:
- BLK1
- BLK2
- BLK3
- BLK4
- BLK5
- BLK6
- BLK7

However, no LT-PIX is forced into seven BLKs.

Internal musical names such as Intro, Verse, Chorus, Bridge, Hook, Tag, and Outro are used only for internal identification, processing, and II alignment.

Buyer-facing and CI-facing language remains clean:
- BLK
- KK
- sK
- package
- K-KUT
- HUG
- CI

- source lineage,
- delivery requirements,

## Priority Package 1: Best Birthday

Best Birthday has two versions.

### Best Birthday — Short

Purpose:
A fast, bright, personal birthday audio moment.

Buyer need:
I want something better than a text, but simple.

Core promise:
A short birthday K-KUT that feels personal, warm, and easy to send.

BOT intake:
- recipient name
- relationship
- birthday tone
- short note from sender
- delivery timing
- optional voice/text dedication

Tone options:
- fun
- sweet
- proud
- funny
- sentimental
- faith-friendly
- bold

Inventory target:
- Short-LT-PIXs
- KKs with birthday-adjacent joy, pride, affection, encouragement, or celebration
- sKs for quick delivery and shareable emotional impact

Suggested package name:
Best Birthday — Short K-KUT

CI trigger:
When selected and purchased, the birthday inventory item becomes a Birthday CI with recipient, sender, tone, date, delivery proof, and internal source lineage.

- relationship
- age or milestone, optional
- tone
- memory or phrase to include
- sender message
- delivery timing
- optional short sK companion

Tone options:

- emotional
- legacy
- faith-friendly

Inventory target:

- KKs for highlight moments
