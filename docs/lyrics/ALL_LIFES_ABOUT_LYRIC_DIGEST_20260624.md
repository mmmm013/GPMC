## Lyric Digest

### Core Subject

A social-conscience song using the ocean/water as a moral mirror.

The lyric contrasts ordinary human struggle against visible wealth, asking whether people have forgotten what life is really about.

### Central Emotional Engine

- Frustration with money-first culture
- Compassion for working people, elders, single parents, and children in poverty
- Moral/spiritual discomfort
- Desire to step back, look at nature, and remember what matters

### Primary Images

- Money being paraded
- Hands with aces
- Avoided answers
- Water / waves / ocean
- Sea grumbling loud
- Old man struggling for groceries
- Checkout girl barely smiling
- Single mom working double shifts
- Wealth beside poverty
- Good Lord as moral witness
- Natural world as corrective truth

### Hook / Title Function

The title phrase **All Life’s About** functions as the moral center.

The chorus turns the water/ocean image into a question:

- Are we proud?
- What is life about?
- What have we forgotten?

### Structure Notes

V1 establishes the money/culture problem.

Ch1 introduces water/ocean as the grounding image and emotional release.

V2 humanizes the problem through specific people.

Ch2 repeats and reinforces the central hook.

Bridge raises the moral/spiritual indictment directly.

Alt Ch3 shifts from observation to personal urgency and public call: “People / Look around.”
