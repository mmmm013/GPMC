# GPMC KKr Two-Lane Doctrine

Date: 2026-06-24  
Doctrine status: Active  
Scope: GPMC / GPMx / KKr / K-KUT / KK / sK / CI operations  

---

## Core Principle

KKr must process LT-PIX material through two distinct lanes:

1. Formal lineage KKs
2. Naked KKs

These lanes serve different purposes.

They must communicate with each other, but they must never be confused.

---

## Critical Boundary

"Naked" means front-end and descriptive detachment.

"Naked" does not mean legal, financial, authorship, publishing, or PRO detachment.

A naked KK may receive its own front-end identity, MetaGrab data, BOT support framing, emotional tags, use-case tags, and product behavior.

However, every naked KK must retain hidden/admin lineage back to the original track, LT-PIX, composition, creator, publisher, PRO, and reporting chain.

Public-facing simplicity must never override admin/legal truth.

---

## Run 1 — Formal Lineage KK Lane

Run 1 creates or reviews KKs tied directly to the LT-PIX and original track/source metadata.

Formal lineage KKs preserve source truth.

### Formal lineage KK requirements

Each Run 1 KK must preserve, as applicable:

- source track
- source PIX
- source LT-PIX
- source section
- section order
- contiguous source range
- composer
- lyricist
- publisher
- PRO / rights reporting basis
- source title / prior title / AKA history
- original track/song content boundary
- applicable legal and financial lineage

### Sales constraint

Formal lineage KKs may be sold as individual KKs.

Formal lineage KK-Kombos may only be sold when the included sections are contiguous in the original track/song.

Non-contiguous song sections must not be joined into one single sold KK-Kombo.

This protects honesty, transparency, ASCAP/reporting integrity, and GPMx internal policy.

---

## Run 2 — Naked KK Lane

Run 2 takes eligible KK material and reprocesses it as naked KK material.

Naked KK processing strips front-end descriptive metadata from Run 1 and re-runs the unit through KKr as its own user-facing object.

The naked KK is treated as a new customer-facing unit for discovery, emotional use, BOT support, package routing, and CI behavior.

### Naked KK requirements

Each naked KK must receive its own:

- system-based unique ID
- MetaGrab record
- emotional tags
- use-case tags
- public-facing support language
- BOT support cues
- package eligibility flags
- customer-facing description
- front-end display treatment

### Naked KK restrictions

A naked KK must not pretend to be legally orphaned.

A naked KK must not lose its admin lineage.

A naked KK must not overwrite or erase the source track, LT-PIX, creator, publisher, PRO, or reporting chain.

A naked KK must not be bundled into a non-contiguous combo that violates the original source-section order.

---

## Sequential Unique ID Rule

Formal lineage KKs and naked KKs must not share identity merely because they share audio or lyric material.

They need related but distinct system identities.

A suitable pattern is:

- Formal lineage KK ID: tied to LT-PIX and source section order
- Naked KK ID: tied to the naked KKr/MetaGrab processing sequence
- CI ID: created only when an inventory item becomes a transaction/cost item

A naked KK may point back to its formal lineage KK, but it is not the same operational object.

---

## Contiguity Rule

Sequence matters for legal and commercial constraints.

When selling tracks, songs, KKs, or KK-Kombos, GPMx must not alter the underlying source content or imply that separated source sections are a single natural excerpt.

A single sold KK-Kombo must use contiguous source sections.

Non-contiguous sections may be separately sold or separately represented, but not joined into one sold source-derived combo unless later cleared by specific legal/business review.

## Back to the Water / Clayton Michael Gunn Note

Back to the Water is a specific reminder that not all works follow the common GDP section pattern.

Back to the Water, with Clayton Michael Gunn identified by admin as composer and lyricist, must not be forced into the common GDP assumption pattern.

Clayton Michael Gunn's writing/section behavior may differ among and between BLKs.

Therefore, when processing Back to the Water into KKs, KKr must register BLKs individually on the naked side.

This protects the work from false structural assumptions.

## GDP Pattern Note

GDP writing often follows a familiar pattern:

- V1
- Ch1
- V2
- Ch2
- Br
- Ch3
- Outro

This is a useful expectation for some GDP-authored works.

It is not a universal KKr rule.

KKr must listen to the actual song structure, lyric function, musical transitions, and BLK behavior.

## Three-Level Operating Model

All KKr and KK/sK/CI work must be evaluated at three levels.

### 1. Big level — GPEx / GPMx

This level governs:

- invention doctrine
- enterprise integrity
- rights strategy
- legal/financial reporting
- catalog architecture
- operational ethics
- long-term scalability

### 2. Middle level — GPMDs

This level governs:

- metadata
- registries
- Track / PIX / LT-PIX / KK / sK processing
- MetaGrab
- triage
- QA
- batch intake
- source control

### 3. Public-facing level — .coms / CIs

This level governs:

- what buyers see
- what buyers choose
- what buyers hear
- what buyers purchase
- what buyers receive
- customer support
- BOT support
- CI behavior

The levels must communicate clearly.

Public-facing simplicity must be supported by middle-layer metadata and big-level legal/ethical integrity.

## Required Hidden/Admin Lineage Fields

Every KK, naked KK, sK, and CI-capable inventory item should eventually carry hidden/admin lineage fields, including:

- source_track_id
- source_pix_id
- source_lt_pix_id
- source_section_id
- source_section_order
- source_section_start
- source_section_end
- contiguous_group_id
- formal_lineage_kk_id
- naked_kk_id, where applicable
- source_title
- prior_title
- aka_title
- composer
- lyricist
- publisher
- PRO
- rights notes
- revenue/reporting lineage
- legal_lineage_id

---

## Required Front-End / Naked Metadata Fields

Naked KKs and customer-facing inventory items may carry:

- display_title
- naked_title
- emotional lane
- use case
- relationship context
- theme tags
- mood tags
- buyer-facing description
- BOT support phrasing
- package eligibility
- CI eligibility

These fields are for front-end usefulness.

They must not replace hidden/admin legal lineage.

---

## CI Boundary

CI means Cost Item.

A KK, naked KK, sK, LT-PIX, or related inventory item becomes a CI only when it enters a purchase, transaction, fulfillment, or cost-accounting state.

Inventory objects are CI-capable before purchase.

They are not CIs until the transaction state exists.

---

## Doctrine Summary

KKr is always two-lane:

Formal lineage lane protects legal/source truth.

Naked KK lane creates independent customer-facing usefulness.

Naked never means legally orphaned.

Combo never means non-contiguous stitching.

Public-facing simplicity never overrides admin/legal truth.

GPMx must remain legal, transparent, forthcoming, internally consistent, and operationally honest.
