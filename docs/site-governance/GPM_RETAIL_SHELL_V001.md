# GPM Retail Shell V001

## Authority

Approved by GD for controlled implementation.

## Purpose

Normalize the existing `mmmm013/GPMC` public application into the first non-selling G Putnam Music retail-song shell without creating a new platform or repository.

## Included scope

- Correct public metadata from K-KUT to G Putnam Music.
- Correct telemetry source identity to `gputnam-music`.
- Replace legacy public navigation with retail-song navigation.
- Add a non-selling `/songs` catalog shell.
- Provide separate shells for approved `IN/O-PIX` and `LT-PIX` records.
- Isolate the former Heart-Tap / Digital Mixed Bag gift checkout.
- Reduce `/hugs` to a separate K-KUT referral.
- Preserve commercial licensing and creator intake as secondary routes.
- Correct public spelling to `KLEIGH`.

## Excluded scope

- No retail pricing.
- No retail checkout.
- No Stripe Product, Price, Payment Link, or Checkout Session changes.
- No public song records connected.
- No first-song release set selected.
- No customer download license created.
- No download format selected.
- No audio mutation.
- No inventory mutation.
- No domain change.
- No production deployment.
- No new GPM platform or public repository.
- No KLEIGH storefront conversion.

## Public domain separation

### `gputnammusic.com`

Complete G Putnam Music song listening and future retail downloads.

### `k-kut.com`

K-KUT, HUG, TUG, and derived music-moment products.

### KLEIGH sites

Held for a later controlled phase. Public and operational spelling is always `KLEIGH`.

## Catalog shell law

Only records that pass the approved GPMC / KKr retail eligibility gate may be connected to the public song catalog.

The shell contains two retained classification lanes:

- `GPM IN/O-PIX`
- `GPM LT-PIX`

The exact public meaning and buyer-facing description of both lanes remain held for GD approval.

## Five unresolved retail locks

1. Exact public meaning and wording of `IN/O-PIX`.
2. Retail file format: MP3, WAV, or both.
3. Retail song price or pricing tiers.
4. Customer download-use license language.
5. Exact first approved song set.

No pricing or checkout may be added until all five locks are resolved.

## BF boundary

BF may control public wording, artwork, grouping, navigation placement, preview treatment, and domain-specific calls to action.

BF may not alter canonical identity, rights, source lineage, approved master, audio hash, artist authority, classification, or approval status.

## Legacy commerce isolation

The former GPM Heart-Tap / Digital Mixed Bag checkout is closed in this shell. `/gift` provides only an informational route to complete songs or the separate K-KUT domain.

## Release state

This document governs a branch implementation only. Merge and production deployment require separate GD approval.
