## Visual treatment

- Full-bleed photography may lead the homepage and route heroes.
- The homepage visual stage may rotate only through the confirmed asset manifest.
- Rotation must include a visible pause/resume control.
- User-selected images pause automatic rotation.
- Every route must preserve readable contrast through controlled overlays.
- Mobile navigation must retain minimum forty-four-pixel touch targets.
- Hover motion must not be required to understand or use the page.
- Photography and artwork support the music catalog; they do not replace catalog identity.

## Identity control

A filename is not identity proof.

- Do not infer or publish a person's name solely from a filename.
- Use neutral alt text until subject identity is confirmed.
- `KLEIGH` is always uppercase.
- Artist, performer, creator, and rights-holder labels require confirmed authority.

## MOV and video hold

The repository history includes a prior larger video-inventory concept, but this branch does not contain an approved current public media manifest.

No MOV or video may enter the public showcase until its manifest row confirms:

- canonical media ID
- source path or controlled public URL
- title
- subject or participants
- duration
- orientation and dimensions
- rights authority
- public-use approval
- poster frame
- destination route or lane
- mute-first and control behavior

Unknown footage must never autoplay.

Public video behavior, when later approved:

- muted by default
- explicit controls
- no automatic sound
- poster frame required
- still-image fallback required
- captions or transcript where speech is material
- reduced-motion preference respected
